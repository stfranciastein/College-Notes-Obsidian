### Business Analytics

There are four types of business analytics.
- Descriptive Analytics.
- Diagnostic Analytics.
- Predictive Analytics.
- Prescriptive Analytics.

***Prescriptive Analytics.*** Provides a recommendation/prescription on what actions to take to achieve some objective goal. uses optimisation techniques as its most useful tool.

- Valuable but not largely used.
- Applicable to almost any industry.
- Provides a focus to answer specific questions.

In the healthcare industry, this can be used to better manage the patient population by using prescriptive analytics to measure the number of patients who are clinically obese, then add filters for factors like diabetes and LDL cholesterol levels to determine where to focus treatment.

***Predictive Analytics.*** An analysis of likely scenarios. Uses big data to identify past patterns to predict the future.

***Diagnostic Analytics.*** Looks at past performance to determine what happened and why. The result of the analysis is often displayed on an analytic dashboard i.e. Google Analytics.

In the retail industry, this is often used when department stores clear seasonal inventory by reducing prices where they need to ask/answer 'When do we reduce things and by how much'.

***Correlation and Causation.*** Relationship between two variables and looking to see if one variable produces an effect in another variable because correlation does not =/= causation.

1) Correlation: Evidence of association between X and Y i.e. Facebook Ad => Traffic Increase.
2) Temporal Antecedence: X must occur before Y i.e. Facebook Ad before Traffic Increase.
3) No Third Factor: Control of other possible factors i.e. an Instagram or X Post.
