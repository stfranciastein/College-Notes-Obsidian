### Intro

***Google Analytics.*** In order to utilise Google analytics, you need to add Google Analytics tags either through Google Tag (gtag.js) or Google Tag manager. Collects interaction data i.e. views, clicks, scrolls, conversions. Interacts with Google Tags to pass onto other Google products. The full documentation can be found at [Tagging for Google Analytics  |  Google for Developers](https://developers.google.com/analytics/devguides/collection/ga4/tag-options).
