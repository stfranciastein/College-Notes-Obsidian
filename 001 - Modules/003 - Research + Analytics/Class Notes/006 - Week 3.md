### Predictive Analytics

***EquiRatings (Ireland).*** uses predictive analytics for equestrian competition outcomes.
1. Relies on millions or rows of historical data.
2. Uses SAP predictive tools.
3. Runs 10,000 simulations per event.

***Use Case Scenario.*** A company enters the data into a linear regression model in order to analyse and prescribe the best probably outcome that can be given to a specific customer i.e. make them purchase a product.

***Call to Action.*** After the website receives a 'prospect' (potential customer), the data taken from here is used by the marketing department. This data is analysed so that they can predict which channel needs to be targeted in order to get the prospect to see the call to action. i.e. offer a specific 'limited time' discount.

***Python Example.*** Will use Jupyter Notebook in order to calculate propensity to buy.
