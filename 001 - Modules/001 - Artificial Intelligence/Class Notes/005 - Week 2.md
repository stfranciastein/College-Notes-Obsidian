### Three Layers of the AI Stack

1. **Application Development.** Providing a model with good prompts/prompt engineering and necessary context. Requires rigorous evaluation and sometimes can require good interfaces.
2. **Model Development.** Dataset engineering and creation/providing of tooling for models.
3. **Infrastructure.** Model serving, data management, computation and monitoring.

***Pre-Training.*** Training a model from scratch. In LLMs, this often involves training the model for text completion. This step takes a very long time to do and can incur a significant loss and setback for the project resource-wise.

***Post-Training.*** Training a model after the pre-training phase. Post-training and fine-training can be used interchangeably but sometimes people might use them differently.

***Dataset Engineering.*** Curating, generating and annotating the data needed for training and adapting AI models. Prior to LLMs, most use cases were close ended as the model could only output predefined values. In foundation models these are open-ended and can provide a wider range of responses. The setback in foundation models is that it's a bigger challenge due to its scope.

***Prompt Engineering.*** Getting AI models to express desirable behaviours from the input alone without changing model weights. It's less about giving the model a specific prompt but giving it the context and tools it needs to do a specific task.
